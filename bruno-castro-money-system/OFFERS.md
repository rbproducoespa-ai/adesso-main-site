# A. HIGH VALUE

## A1 — Exhibition stand design, concept to production

**Ideal client.** Small and mid-size stand builders and event agencies in the
UK, Germany, Netherlands, Spain and the UAE, without a full-time designer or
without capacity during show season.

**Problem solved.** They win the job, then cannot draw it fast enough — or their
designer produces something the workshop then has to redraw.

**Deliverables.** Two concept directions · client-ready 3D renders · floor plan ·
production drawings · panel schedules · print-ready artwork · revisions during
build.

**Price** £1,400–3,200 · **Floor** £1,100 · **Timeline** 5–8 working days
**Upsell** → A5 full show package · **Recurring** → C1 overflow retainer

**Why you win it.** You have installed what you draw. Say that sentence in the
first call; it is the entire pitch.

---

## A2 — beMatrix modular stand design

**Ideal client.** beMatrix bePartners and beMasters — a finite, named, findable
list. See `day-1/prospects.csv`.

**Problem solved.** Designing in beMatrix properly means knowing frame sizes,
infill logic and rental stock. Generic designers specify things that cannot be
built from the system, and the builder eats the cost.

**Deliverables.** Everything in A1, plus frame specification, infill schedule,
rental-stock-aware design, and LEDskin integration with pitch and panel layout.

**Price** £1,800–4,000 · **Floor** £1,500 · **Timeline** 5–8 working days
**Upsell** → A4 LED support · **Recurring** → C1

**Why you win it.** This is your most defensible offer. The pool of people who
can do beMatrix *and* LEDskin *and* production drawings is tiny. Price at the
top of the band, not the middle.

## A3 — White-label design for agencies

**Ideal client.** Design and event agencies that need capacity under their own
brand, invisibly.

**Problem solved.** They have more won work than desk hours during Q1 and Q3
show season, and hiring is too slow.

**Deliverables.** Work delivered in the agency's template, their title block,
their file naming. Your name appears nowhere. NDA as standard.

**Price** £380/day, minimum two days · **Floor** £320/day
**Upsell** → C1 retainer after the second engagement

**Why you win it.** Agencies buy reliability, not brilliance. Hit two deadlines
in a row and you become the default call.

---

## A4 — LEDskin diagnostics and repair consulting

**Ideal client.** Stand builders and AV contractors with LED walls that fail
during build-up, when there is no time and no spare.

**Problem solved.** A dead panel at 6pm the night before a show opens is a
five-figure problem. Almost nobody can diagnose it remotely.

**Deliverables.** Remote diagnosis from photos and video · repair procedure ·
parts identification · escalation guidance · optional on-site attendance.

**Price** £80–150/h remote · £400–700/day on-site · **Floor** £80/h
**Timeline** Same day, that is the product
**Upsell** → A2 design work · **Recurring** → C6 support contract

**Why you win it.** Nobody else answers the phone at 6pm with the right answer.
This is a relationship opener disguised as a service: the client who lets you
fix their LED wall will let you design their next stand.

## A5 — Full show package: stand plus digital layer

**Ideal client.** Brands exhibiting directly, particularly Brazilian companies
exhibiting in Europe — where being a native Portuguese speaker based in the UK
removes all the friction.

**Problem solved.** They spend £40–80k on a stand, collect business cards in a
bowl, and never follow up. The commercial layer is missing entirely.

**Deliverables.** Everything in A1, plus a show landing page, QR lead capture,
automated follow-up sequence, and a post-show lead report.

**Price** £2,400–6,000 · **Floor** £2,000 · **Timeline** 3–4 weeks
**Recurring** → C2 or C5 after the show

**Why you win it.** Nobody else sells both halves. This is the offer that only
you can make, and it is why the four skills belong on one invoice.

## A6 — Internal business system

**Ideal client.** Companies of 3 to 30 people running operations on
spreadsheets, WhatsApp and memory. Workshops, contractors, agencies, local
service businesses.

**Problem solved.** The owner is the system. Nothing is visible, nothing is
repeatable, and everything stops when they are away.

**Deliverables.** CRM and pipeline · shared inbox · job and order tracking ·
WhatsApp flows · dashboards · roles · documentation · handover.

**Price** £1,600–5,000 · **Floor** £1,400 · **Timeline** 3–6 weeks
**Recurring** → C4 maintenance, always attached

**Why you win it.** You built one for yourself and it works. Demo the real
thing; a live system beats any deck.

## A7 — AI workflow automation

**Ideal client.** Businesses already paying for tools that do not talk to each
other.

**Problem solved.** Manual copying between systems, follow-ups that never
happen, quotes that take an afternoon.

**Deliverables.** Mapped process · n8n or agent workflows · API integrations ·
monitoring · documentation.

**Price** simple £400 · medium £900 · complex £2,500 · **Floor** £250
**Timeline** 3 days to 3 weeks · **Recurring** → C4

**Sell the outcome, never the technology.** "I remove the two hours a day your
team spends copying between systems" closes. "I work with n8n and AI agents"
does not.

# C. RECURRING REVENUE

The difference between a freelancer and a business. Every high-value delivery
should end with a recurring offer attached.

## C1 — Exhibition overflow retainer
Agreed monthly capacity for a stand builder or agency: a set number of design
days reserved each month, at a lower effective rate in exchange for the
commitment. **£800–2,400/mo.** Best sold in December and June, before Q1 and Q3
show season.

## C2 — Marketing and ads management
Campaign management, creative refresh, monthly reporting. **£400–1,200/mo.**

## C3 — Website care plan
Hosting, updates, backups, monitoring, small changes. **£80–250/mo.** Attach to
every B1. Never deliver a website without offering it.

## C4 — Automation maintenance
Monitoring, fixes, changes as the business changes. **£150–400/mo.** Attach to
every A6 and A7.

## C5 — Lead management as a service
You run their pipeline: chase leads, follow up, book meetings, report. **£300–800/mo.**
Highest margin of any offer here because the system does the work.

## C6 — LED support contract
Priority response during build-up weeks, remote diagnosis included, on-site at
an agreed rate. **£200–600/mo.** Sell to any builder who has ever lost a day to
a dead panel.

## Pricing discipline

1. **Never quote hours.** Fixed scope, fixed price, stated timeline.
2. **Never go below the floor.** The floor is not a negotiating position.
3. **Always name the recurring offer** in the same conversation as the project.
4. **Raise prices after every third win** in a category. If nobody is saying no, the price is too low.
5. **Deposit 50% up front** on anything above £1,000. Non-negotiable.

## Positioning line

> Freelance Exhibition Designer and Technical Creative Specialist, available for
> white-label and project support across the UK and Europe.

Use it verbatim in outreach. It names the service, the model and the geography
in one sentence, which is all a busy agency reads.

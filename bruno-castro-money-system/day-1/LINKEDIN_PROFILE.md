# LinkedIn profile — copy and paste

Thirty minutes of work. Do it before any outreach, because every person you
contact will look at the profile before replying.

**The principle behind every choice below:** LinkedIn search is literal. Clients
and recruiters type job titles, not concepts. "Creative Technologist" is a good
idea and a bad search term — it positions you well to someone already reading
your profile, and makes you invisible to everyone who has not found you yet. So
job titles go first, positioning goes second.

---

## 1. Headline

LinkedIn allows 220 characters. Use them — every word is indexed.

### Recommended

```
Exhibition & 3D Designer | beMatrix & LEDskin Specialist | Technical Drawings, Visualisation & Production | AI Automation | London · Remote Worldwide
```

148 characters. Why this order:

- **Exhibition & 3D Designer** — the exact phrase agencies search
- **beMatrix & LEDskin Specialist** — almost nobody else can claim it, and beMatrix partners search the term
- **Technical Drawings, Visualisation & Production** — three more searchable phrases, and "Production" signals you know the workshop
- **AI Automation** — the second stream, present but not competing
- **London · Remote Worldwide** — geography, because agencies filter by it

### Alternative A — for agency and white-label work

```
Freelance Exhibition Designer | White-Label Stand Design, beMatrix, Technical & Production Drawings | Available for Agency Overflow | UK & Europe
```

Use this one if the exhibition overflow retainer (offer C1) becomes the focus.
"Available for Agency Overflow" is the phrase that makes an agency owner stop.

### Alternative B — automation-first

```
AI Automation & Business Systems Developer | n8n, Next.js, CRM & WhatsApp Automation | I remove the manual work from small businesses | London
```

Only use this if you decide automation leads. Do not try to lead with both.

## 4. Skills

LinkedIn shows the top three and weights them heavily in search. Pin these, in
this order:

1. **Exhibition Design**
2. **SketchUp**
3. **AutoCAD**

Then add, in roughly this order of value to you:

```
beMatrix · 3D Visualisation · Technical Drawing · Autodesk Inventor · Twinmotion
Exhibition Stands · Spatial Design · Production Drawings · LED Displays
Large Format Printing · Prepress · Adobe Illustrator · Adobe Photoshop · InDesign
After Effects · Premiere Pro · Next.js · TypeScript · Supabase · n8n
Workflow Automation · Meta Ads · Google Ads · SEO · Live Sound · Audio Engineering
Pro Tools · System Alignment
```

Ask three former colleagues to endorse the first three. Endorsements on the
pinned skills move search ranking more than the rest of the profile combined.

## 5. Settings that matter

- [ ] **Custom URL** — `linkedin.com/in/brunocastrodesign` or similar. Goes on every proposal and email signature.
- [ ] **Open to work → Services** — select Graphic Design, Web Design, Marketing. Recruiters and clients filter on this. Use the "Services" badge rather than the green "Open to Work" photo frame, which reads as unemployed rather than available.
- [ ] **Services page** — LinkedIn generates a free mini profile that appears in its own search surface. Fill it in.
- [ ] **Banner image** — a render of your best stand, or a stand under build. Not an abstract gradient. This is the only place on LinkedIn where your actual work is visible before a click.
- [ ] **Profile photo** — face, neutral background, no sunglasses. If you have one on site in hi-vis, that works better than a studio portrait for this industry.
- [ ] **Featured section** — pin the portfolio site, one beMatrix project, one LEDskin project.
- [ ] **Location** — set to London, not a suburb. Agencies filter by city.

## 6. First week of posting

Three posts. Not to build an audience — to give the people you cold-contact
something to see when they check you out.

**Post 1 — the credibility post.** A stand you designed, beside a photo of it
built. Caption: what was difficult, and how the drawing solved it. Two hundred
words maximum.

**Post 2 — the rare skill post.** A LEDskin fault you diagnosed and fixed. This
is the post almost nobody else in the industry can write. It will reach further
than anything else you publish.

**Post 3 — the availability post.**

```
I have capacity for two exhibition design projects in [MONTH].

Concept, 3D, technical and production drawings — including beMatrix
specification. I work white-label for agencies, in your templates, under NDA.

Ten years on European exhibition floors means the drawings build. If you are
short on desk hours this show season, message me.
```

Do not post more than three times in the first week. The profile is the asset;
the posts only exist so it does not look dormant.

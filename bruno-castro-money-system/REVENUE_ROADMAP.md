## The activity maths

Working backwards from three exhibition wins per month.

```
400  contacts per month        (20/day × 20 working days)
 ↓   10% reply rate            — with a free 3D concept attached
 40  replies
 ↓   40% qualify
 16  qualified conversations
 ↓   60% reach proposal
 10  proposals sent
 ↓   30% win rate
  3  wins
```

Three wins at an average of £1,800 is £5,400 — the exhibition stream, met.

**The 10% reply rate is doing the heavy lifting and it is not the industry
norm.** Cold email to agencies typically returns 2–4%. It gets to 10% only
because of the free 3D concept: you send a stand builder a concept for a show
they are actually working, produced in ninety minutes. Almost nobody does this.

If your reply rate comes in at 4%, the maths gives 1 win, not 3. That is the
number to watch in week two — and the fix is the quality of the concept, not
the volume of the emails.

## 90 days

### Days 1–30 — Foundation and first cash

Targets: **£1,200–2,500**

- Day 1: LinkedIn rewritten. Prospect list started.
- Days 2–5: 200 prospects loaded, beMatrix partners flagged.
- Days 3–7: portfolio live, six case studies written.
- Days 5–30: outreach begins and does not stop. 20 contacts/day.
- Register on YunoJuno and Twine at a declared £380/day. Upwork and Contra for automation.
- Take on-site work — install and LED preferentially, since it pays *and* prospects.

First money most likely comes from fast services and on-site work. That is fine.
Its job is to buy you the patience not to underprice the exhibition work.

### Days 31–60 — First real deals

Targets: **£2,500–4,500**

- First exhibition package delivered.
- First recurring client signed. Every delivery ends with a recurring offer.
- Reply rates measured by segment. Double down on what answers, drop what does not.
- One beMatrix or LEDskin case study published — the highest-leverage content you can produce.

### Days 61–90 — Repeatable

Targets: **£4,000–7,000**

- Two exhibition packages plus overflow days.
- Three or more recurring clients.
- First C1 overflow retainer with an agency.
- Prices raised in any category with three wins.
- Physical work capped at two days per week.

**By day 90 the question changes** from "where does the next job come from" to
"which of these do I stop doing". `MASTER_STATUS.md` P13 handles that.

**Read this carefully:** Adesso scores lowest on revenue now and highest on
potential. That is not a reason to abandon it — it is the visa business and the

the day.

LED Repair UK is underrated in this table. It scores Priority B on the numbers,
but strategically it is the cheapest door into the Priority A exhibition
clients. Treat it as marketing that pays for itself.
